About this policy

SoftBear ("we", "us") publishes mobile games on Google Play. This policy applies to all of our games and to this website. It explains what information is handled when you play our games, why, and the choices you have.

Some details differ between games — for example, whether a game offers cloud saves or sign-in. Those differences are listed under Game-specific details.

For privacy questions, support, or data requests, contact softbears.game@gmail.com.

Information handled by our games

  • Game progress and settings: levels, unlocked content, in-game currency, missions, rewards, and preferences are stored on your device. In games that offer cloud services, a copy of your save and related metadata can be stored using Unity Cloud Save.
  • Player and account information: in games that use Unity Authentication, a player identifier is assigned, including for guest sessions. Sign-in providers, where enabled, process the identifiers and authentication information needed to link or restore your game. Cloud-save metadata can include a player ID, save timestamps and revision, a device description, and progress summaries. Do not send us your account password or sign-in codes.
  • Advertising and technical information: our games use Google AdMob to show ads, such as rewarded ads. Its SDK may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, advertising and other device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. These are used for advertising, measurement, service operation, and fraud prevention, subject to applicable privacy choices.
  • Support messages: if you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include so we can respond to your request.

Why information is used

Information is used to run our games, save and restore progress, authenticate players, provide and measure ads, apply privacy choices, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support and privacy requests.

Service providers

Google AdMob provides advertising. Unity Gaming Services provides authentication and cloud-save functionality in the games that use it. Available sign-in providers handle their sign-in processes under their own policies. These services may process information in countries other than your own.

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Unity Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We may also disclose information where required by applicable law or necessary to address security or fraud, subject to applicable requirements.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local game data is retained on your device until you remove it using Android app-storage controls or other applicable device controls. Removing local game data does not by itself delete an existing cloud save.

Cloud game data is retained to provide account and save-restoration functionality. You can request deletion through a game's account controls, where available, or by contacting us as described below. Data processed by service providers, including their operational records or backups, is subject to their retention practices and applicable legal requirements. This policy does not promise an immediate purge of every provider record.

Request account or data deletion

Games with cloud-account features include an in-game option to delete your game account — see Game-specific details.

You can also request deletion without opening or reinstalling a game. Email softbears.game@gmail.com with the subject [Game name] — Data deletion.

Include your player ID, if available, and enough information to identify the game account. Explain whether you want the account and cloud progress deleted or have another data request. Never send passwords or authentication codes. We may request information necessary to verify ownership before acting. We will explain any information that must be retained for applicable legal or security reasons.

Deleting a game account does not automatically delete records that Google or other providers control independently, and it does not delete your separate Google or other sign-in-provider account. Their policies explain their own request procedures.

Privacy choices

Where available, a game's settings let you review advertising consent choices. Availability depends on your region and the advertising consent system. Android may also provide advertising-privacy controls, such as resetting or deleting your advertising ID. If you choose not to watch a rewarded ad, its associated reward is not earned.

Children

Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's information or believe a child has provided personal information can contact softbears.game@gmail.com. Please identify the game and provide enough information for us to investigate. We review these requests and address information that must be removed under applicable requirements. Do not send unnecessary information about a child in a support message.

Security and this website

Our games use network services for authentication, cloud saves, and advertising. No method of storage or transmission provides an absolute security guarantee.

This website does not include our own analytics scripts, advertising, contact forms, or player sign-in features. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information, including visitor IP addresses, to provide and secure its hosting service — see the GitHub Privacy Statement. Web fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser information when the page loads — see the Google Privacy Policy.

Game-specific details

The sections above apply to every game. The details below describe features specific to individual games.

Food Street: Idle Tycoon

Also known as Idle Food Street · Android package com.softbear.idlefoodstreet

  • Game data: unlocked streets and stalls, levels, in-game currency, missions, rewards, staff assignments, and preferences are stored on your device. When cloud services are available, a copy of your save is stored using Unity Cloud Save.
  • Account: Unity Authentication assigns a player identifier, including for guest sessions. Sign-in providers, where enabled, can be used to link or restore your game.
  • Ads: Google AdMob rewarded ads.
  • Delete account in the game: where cloud-account features are available, select DELETE ACCOUNT in the game's account screen to delete the current game account and its cloud save. The game can then continue as a guest while retaining this device's local progress. To remove local progress too, use Android's app-storage controls.
  • Delete by email: use the subject Food Street: Idle Tycoon — Data deletion.
  • Ad privacy: where available, SETTINGS → AD PRIVACY lets you review advertising consent choices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its update date when the policy changes. Contact softbears.game@gmail.com for privacy questions, support, and requests about your information.
